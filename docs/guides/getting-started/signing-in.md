# Signing in

## Your account

Most of our sites use **one shared login** (called Authelia). Your account is created by the person who runs the server, and it works on every site that asks you to sign in.

When you open a site you may be sent to a login page at `auth.f9.casa`.

1. Enter your username and password.
2. Approve the **second step** (a code from your authenticator app, or the method you set up).
3. You're taken straight back to the site you wanted.

!!! info "You only do this now and then"
You stay signed in for a while on each device, so you won't see the login page every time.

## First time setup

1. Ask for an account if you don't have one.
2. Open any of our sites, such as <https://f9.casa>.
3. Sign in with the details you were given and follow the prompts to set up the second step.
4. Change your password if you were given a temporary one.

## Forgot your password?

On the login page choose **Reset password**. If the email doesn't arrive, ask the person who runs the server.

## Add a shortcut to your phone

=== "iPhone"
Open the site in Safari, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**.

=== "Android"
Open the site in Chrome, tap the **three dots**, then **Add to Home screen** (or **Install app**).

## Why can't I open something?

Access depends on the group your account is in. Day-to-day sites such as photos, documents, the smart home and AI chat are open to everyone in the house. Some admin tools, such as the download managers, are only for the person who runs the server. If you get an "access denied" page for something you need, ask.
