# Photos (Immich)

**Address:** <https://photos.f9.casa>

Immich is our private version of Google Photos. Your photos and videos stay on our own server, not with a big company.

## Set up on your phone

1. Install the **Immich** app from the App Store or Play Store.
2. For the server address, enter `https://photos.f9.casa`.
3. Sign in with your Immich account (ask for one if you don't have it).
4. Open **Backup** in the app and turn it on, choosing which albums to back up.

New photos then upload automatically, usually when you're on Wi-Fi.

## Finding photos

- **Search** understands plain descriptions, such as "dog on the beach" or "birthday cake".
- **Explore** groups photos by people and places.
- Use **Albums** to collect photos and share them with others in the house.

## Tips

- Deleting from the app can also remove it from your phone. Check the confirmation.
- Photos go in a trash for a while before being permanently removed.
- Backing up large videos is faster on Wi-Fi with the phone charging.
