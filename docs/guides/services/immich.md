# Photos (Immich)

**Address:** <https://photos.f9.casa>

**Works:** anywhere, at home or away.

Immich is our private version of Google Photos. Your photos and videos stay on our own server, not with a big company.

## Set up on your phone

1. Install the **Immich** app:

<a href="https://apps.apple.com/app/id1613945652"><img src="../../assets/badges/app-store.svg" alt="Download on the App Store"></a>
<a href="https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=app.alextran.immich"><img src="../../assets/badges/google-play.png" alt="Get it on Google Play"></a>
</p>
2. For the server address, enter `https://photos.f9.casa`.
3. Choose to sign in with **OAuth / SSO** (or the house login button) and use your house login.
4. Open **Backup** in the app and turn it on, choosing which albums to back up.

New photos then upload automatically, usually when you're on Wi-Fi.

## Finding photos

- **Search** understands plain descriptions, such as "dog on the beach" or "birthday cake".
- **Explore** groups photos by people and places.
- Use **Albums** to collect photos and share them with others in the house.

## Backups

The photos and videos on our server are copied to **Backblaze**, a cloud storage company, **every night**, so they survive even if the server is lost. The copy is encrypted before it is uploaded: the contents of the files are scrambled and Backblaze can't read them. The file names are not hidden. The copy mirrors the library, so something you permanently delete here disappears from the cloud copy the next night.

## Tips

- Deleting from the app can also remove it from your phone. Check the confirmation.
- Photos go in a trash for a while before being permanently removed.
- Backing up large videos is faster on Wi-Fi with the phone charging.
