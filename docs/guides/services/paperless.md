# Documents (Paperless)

**Address:** <https://docs.f9.casa>

Paperless is our digital filing cabinet. Letters, bills, manuals and warranties are scanned in, read automatically and made searchable.

## Find a document

1. Open the site and sign in.
2. Type words from the document into the search box, such as a company name or "boiler warranty".
3. Click a result to read it or download it.

You can also browse by **Correspondent** (who it's from), **Document type** and **Tags**.

## Add a document

The easiest way is the scanner. See [Scanning](scanning.md): put the paper in and press the **Scan** button. It appears in Paperless a minute or so later.

You can also drag a PDF or photo onto the Paperless page to upload it.

## Suggestions

When you open a document, Paperless can suggest a title, tags and a correspondent. Review the suggestions and accept the good ones. The AI runs on our own server, so nothing is sent to the internet.

## Tips

- Search matches the text inside documents, not just their names.
- Don't throw away originals you legally need to keep.
