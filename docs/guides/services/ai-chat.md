# AI chat (Open WebUI)

**Address:** <https://ai.f9.casa>

Open WebUI is a ChatGPT-style assistant that runs on our own server, so your conversations stay in the house.

## Using it

1. Open the site and choose to sign in with **Home**. This uses your house login.
2. Pick a model from the drop-down at the top.
3. Type a question and press enter.

## What it's good for

- Explaining things, drafting emails, summarising text.
- Brainstorming, recipes, quick code snippets.
- Searching the web (when that feature is turned on for the chat).

## Good to know

- It runs on a home graphics card, so busy moments can make it slower than the big online services.
- It can be wrong with confidence. Double-check anything important.
- Conversations are saved to your account. Delete any you don't want to keep.
