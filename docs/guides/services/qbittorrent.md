# Torrent downloads (qBittorrent)

**Address:** <https://torrents.f9.casa>

qBittorrent is the torrent downloader. Like SABnzbd, it is mostly used automatically by the film and TV tools, so most people never need to open it.

!!! warning "Access is limited"
    You need to be in the **downloads** group to open this site. If you see "access denied" and think you should have access, ask Scott.

## What you might do here

- **Check progress.** Active downloads show their percentage, speed and time left.
- **Pause or resume.** Select a download and use the pause or play button.
- **See what's finished.** Use the **Completed** filter on the left.

## Please

- Don't delete downloads or change settings unless Scott asked you to. Seeding after a download finishes is intentional.
- Request things through [Seerr](seerr.md) instead of adding torrents by hand.
