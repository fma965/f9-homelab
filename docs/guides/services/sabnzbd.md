# Usenet downloads (SABnzbd)

**Address:** <https://sabnzbd.f9.casa>

SABnzbd is the downloader for Usenet. Films and TV requested through [Seerr](seerr.md) are sent to it automatically, so most people never need to open it.

!!! warning "Access is limited"
    You need to be in the **downloads** group to open this site. If you see "access denied" and think you should have access, ask Scott.

## What you might do here

- **Check progress.** The main page lists what's downloading, with speed and time left.
- **See what's finished.** The **History** tab lists completed downloads.
- **Pause or resume.** Use the pause button at the top if the internet is needed for something else.

## Please

- Don't delete or change settings. They are tied to the automatic downloading of films and TV.
- Request things through Seerr instead of adding downloads by hand.
