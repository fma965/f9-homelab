# Cameras

**Address:** <https://cctv.f9.casa>

**Works:** at home only, on the house Wi-Fi or plugged in at home.

!!! info "Away from home"
    This site does not open on mobile data or other Wi-Fi. To check the cameras while you are out, use the **Home Assistant** app instead: it can show them too.

Our cameras are managed by Frigate, which records when it sees people, cars or animals and keeps the clips.

## Using it

1. Open the site and confirm with your passkey on the F9 sign-in page. You can watch live and recorded video; the settings are for Scott.
2. **Live** shows the cameras now.
3. **Review** (or **Events**) lists clips from the past few days. Choose one to watch it.

## Please remember

- Camera footage is private to the household. Don't share it without the people in it knowing.
- Clips are kept for a limited time, so download anything you want to keep.
- Home Assistant can also show the cameras on its dashboards.
