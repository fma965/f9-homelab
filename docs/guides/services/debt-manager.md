# Debt Manager

**Address:** <https://debtmanager.f9.casa>

Debt Manager keeps track of money owed and who has paid. Payments are matched automatically from the bank using the **payment reference**, so nobody has to tick things off by hand.

## Using it

1. Open the site and choose **Sign in with Discord**.
2. See what you owe and your payment history.
3. When you pay, use the **reference shown for you** exactly as written.

## Tips

- If the reference is missing or wrong, the payment can't be matched automatically. Tell Scott and he'll sort it out.
- It can take a little while for a payment to show up after the bank processes it.
