# Household inventory (Homebox)

**Address:** <https://inventory.f9.casa>

!!! warning "Work in progress"
Homebox is still being filled in. Many of our things aren't in it yet, so an empty search doesn't mean we don't have it. Scott is adding our products and sorting out the locations.

Homebox is a catalogue of what we own and where it lives: tools, cables, spare parts, appliances, warranties and receipts.

## Find something

1. Open the site and sign in with your house login.
2. Search by name, or browse by **Location** such as "Garage" or "Loft".
3. Open an item to see where it is, its details and any attached manual or receipt.

## Add something

1. Press **Create** and choose **Item**.
2. Give it a name, a location and (optionally) a photo, serial number and warranty date.
3. Save it.

## Tips

- Put things back where Homebox says they live.
- When you move something, update its location so the next person can find it.
- Add the receipt or manual as an attachment so warranty claims are easy.
