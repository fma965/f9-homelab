# Troubleshooting

## A site won't load

1. Check the [status page](status.md).
2. Try again in a minute. Services restart for updates.
3. Check your own internet or Wi-Fi works.
4. Try a private/incognito window. If it works there, clear your browser data for that site.

## It keeps asking me to log in

Make sure cookies are enabled. If it still happens, sign out of Authelia (`auth.f9.casa`) and sign in again.

## "Access denied" or "Forbidden"

Your account isn't in the group that site needs. Ask Scott.

## Printing

- Is the printer on, awake and loaded with paper?
- Is the page showing in the print queue? If so, wait a minute for it to warm up.
- Still nothing? Tell Scott which device you printed from and about what time.

## Scanning

- Is the scanner switched on, with paper properly in the feeder?
- Wait a couple of minutes. Paperless reads the text in the background before the document appears.
- If it doesn't show up, tell Scott what time you scanned.

## Photos aren't backing up

- Open the Immich app and check **Backup** is switched on.
- The phone may be waiting for Wi-Fi or charging. Check the app's backup settings.
- Make sure the app has permission to see all your photos.

## Smart home device isn't responding

- Check the device has power and is on Wi-Fi.
- Try again from the Home Assistant app.
- Tell Scott if it keeps happening.

## Who to ask

Scott. Please say **which site**, **what you did** and **what you saw** (a screenshot helps).
